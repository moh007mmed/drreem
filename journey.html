My Journey

Dental school compresses a lot of growth into a few years. Here's roughly how mine has gone so far.

01
Year 1 · Foundations

Where all of it starts

Anatomy, physiology, biochemistry — building the science underneath everything else. This is also when I learned how much there was still to learn.

02
Year 2 · Pre-Clinical Sciences

First handpiece, first typodont

Dental materials, early simulation labs, and the first time holding a handpiece. Mistakes were made — on typodonts, thankfully, not patients.

03
Year 3 · Clinical Exposure Begins

Meeting real patients

First real patients, under close supervision. Diagnosis, treatment planning, and learning to explain procedures in plain language, not textbook language.

04
Year 4 · Advanced Clinical Practice

More independence, more complexity

More independent casework, more complex procedures, and the start of proper case documentation and research.

Ongoing · Staying Current

Where it's headed next

Conferences, journal articles, and a genuine interest in where digital dentistry and AI-assisted diagnostics are headed next.
