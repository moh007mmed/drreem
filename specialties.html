Areas of Interest

I'm early in my career, so I'll be honest — this list will keep changing. These are the specialties I find myself reading about, asking my professors about, and seeking extra clinical exposure in, tagged the way I'd tag them on an actual chart.

Q1–Q4 · POSTERIOR

Restorative Dentistry

Fillings, crowns, and everything involved in rebuilding a tooth's function. I like that it's part science, part craftsmanship.

ANTERIOR · #6–11

Cosmetic Dentistry

Where confidence and clinical skill meet. A small change here can shift how someone shows up in the world.

FULL ARCH

Orthodontics

Slow, deliberate movement over months and years — I find the biomechanics genuinely fascinating.

PRIMARY DENTITION

Pediatric Dentistry

A different kind of patience required, a different kind of reward. Helping a nervous kid relax in the chair is its own skill.

SURGICAL

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

The steepest learning curve so far. I've only scratched the surface, and I want a lot more of it.

PULP · ROOT CANAL

Endodontics

Precision work most patients never see. I like problems that reward a clear, careful solution.
