About Me

The Short Version

Why I'm doing this, in plain terms

I'm Mohammed — currently in my clinical years of dental school, splitting my time between lecture halls, the student clinic, and, if I'm honest, too many late nights reading case studies. Before dentistry, I worked as a freelance web developer and built AI automation tools for small businesses, which is probably why I think about patient care the way I think about a good process: every step should have a reason.

What drew me to dentistry wasn't a single moment — it was noticing how much trust a patient hands over the second they sit in that chair, and wanting to be someone worth that trust. I'm still building the skills to fully live up to it, but I take the word "still" seriously: I show up, I ask questions, and I don't pretend to know more than I do.

  • Hands-on clinical training under supervision
  • Evidence-based approach to every case
  • Comfortable with digital dentistry & AI tools
  • Clear, patient-first communication

How I Try to Work

Three things I keep coming back to

Comfort First

A calm patient is easier to treat well. I try to explain before I do — never the other way around.

Precision Over Speed

Dentistry rewards patience. Given the choice, I'd rather get it right than get it fast.

Keep Learning

Techniques and evidence change. I try to stay a student even outside the classroom.
